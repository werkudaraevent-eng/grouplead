# LeadEngine design rules

The CRM follows Material Design 3 as rules and logic (structure, spacing,
hierarchy, colour, how a control behaves), never as a visual copy, with
global platforms (HubSpot, Salesforce, Linear, Notion) as references for
patterns Material leaves open. The sibling app records the same discipline
in `apps/sales-mission/DESIGN.md`; the two apps share a session, a colour
system and a set of shell behaviours, and should read as one product.

| Area | Rule (and where it comes from) | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Tokens, not hex | Every colour is a token from `app/globals.css` (M3 tonal surfaces: `--background` surface, `--muted` surface-container-low, `--card` surface-container-lowest, `--sidebar` the navigation panel only, which the person can switch to dark independently of the content, so nothing outside the drawer and its menus may use it, `--border` outline-variant, `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--accent`, `--warning`, `--success`). A raw hex or Tailwind palette colour in a component is a bug, because it cannot follow a theme or a rebrand | `components/ui/*`, `components/shared/*` |
| Form fields have a visible edge | `--input`, the outline role shadcn's `border-input` reads, was #ffffff, so every text field, select, textarea and the list search drew a white edge on a white card and read as bare text. M3's outlined text field puts its outline at the outline role, 3:1 against the surface (WCAG 1.4.11): #7F8993 (3.56:1 on the card), with `--field` #F3F5F8 as the resting fill one step off the card, and the primary as the focus ring. The same two tokens and the same fill as Sales Activity, so a field looks the same in both apps | `--input` / `--field` in `app/globals.css`; `bg-field` on `components/ui/{input,textarea,select}.tsx` and `SearchField` |
| List pages | Page header with the primary action filled and the secondary ones (Export with the count it will write, Import) as labelled outlined buttons beside it, the way Sales Activity's lists carry them; then one toolbar: the search field (outlined, 8dp corners, leading icon, trailing clear, debounced; the same field as Sales Activity's) followed by the filter chips, "+ Filter" as a text button and "Clear all" as a text link, all in one group that wraps onto a second line when there are more filters than room; view tools (save view, columns) stay 40dp icon buttons at the top right of the first line. The columns button carries no badge: an M3 badge flags something new or needing attention, not a setting, so "5 of 13 shown" is said inside its menu, where each column is a checkbox with a drag handle (M3 menu with checkboxes; Gmail, HubSpot, Airtable). An applied filter is always in sight: a sideways-scrolling rail cut chips at its edge and could hide the very filter that emptied the list, which M3 rules out for a chip set on a wide screen (wrap there; scroll only on a narrow one). Saved views appear as choice chips only once a view exists; the first is saved from the toolbar (M3 text field, filter chips, buttons; Linear, Attio, Sales Activity) | `ListPageHeader` actions on the Contacts and Companies pages, `ListToolbar`, `SearchField`, `FilterBuilder`, `ToolbarIconButton`, `ColumnsMenu` (checkbox list, no badge), `SaveViewButton`, `SavedViewsBar` |
| Data table | Header on surface-container-low, 52dp rows, one hairline between rows, hover as a tonal wash, selection as a primary tint, frozen leading columns with one edge shadow, the row's actions as a trailing round icon button that appears on hover or focus, a fixed-height footer with the range on the left and paging on the right (M3 data table, list item, pagination). A selection belongs to the page it was made on and clears on a page change (Gmail), so a bulk action never reaches rows nobody can see; the header checkbox reads this page only, indeterminate when part of it is ticked. A "Has …" filter is a true/false field with the boolean operators | `components/ui/table.tsx`, `components/shared/list-table.tsx`, `ListFooter`, `InitialsAvatar`, `NeedsDetailsBadge` |
| Chips | Every chip has 8dp corners, never a pill (M3 chips; Sales Activity's rule "Chips are M3 chips, not pills"). A filter chip is outlined while it does nothing and, once it narrows the list, takes a primary tint (`border-primary/50 bg-primary/5`) with a leading check and reads "Label: value", with an ✕ to drop it; the same 36px chip as Sales Activity's facet buttons. Choice chips (saved views, roles) are the same shape, tonal with a check on the one selected | `filter-builder.tsx`, `saved-views-bar.tsx`, `settings/permissions/page.tsx` |
| A field reading is a badge, signed | A judgement sent from the field (a contact's DISC communication style) sits in Contact Information as a small tonal badge (`bg-primary/10 text-primary`, the filter-chip tint) with its meaning beside it, the rep's note under it in their own words, and who assessed it and when in the meta size; it is one person's impression on one day, so it is never rendered as a status or a fact, and it is replaced by a newer reading rather than merged (Crystal Knows and Humantic AI beside a HubSpot or Salesforce contact; M3 assist chip, supporting text) | `DiscRow` / `readDisc` in `features/contacts/components/contact-detail-page.tsx`, written by `POST /api/v1/contacts` into `custom_fields.disc` |
| The AI bill is shown where the connection is set, in tokens, as a pace | Every call to the proxy from either app writes one line to a shared log (app, feature, model, tokens in and out, ok), and Settings → AI → Usage (its own page off a header button, because a form ends at its Save and a read-only ledger is another task; OpenAI's and Anthropic's consoles keep Usage beside the keys, not under them) shows: the last 7 and 30 days as calls and tokens, then what a week and a month cost at the last week's pace, then the split per feature and per model, because the person paying wants the monthly token figure before the invoice, not after (OpenAI's and Anthropic's usage pages, Vercel AI usage, Salesforce's Einstein request consumption all sit beside the credentials). Tokens, never currency: the price is the proxy's and would go stale here. A log younger than a week projects from the days it covers, an empty log says so instead of showing zeros, and failed calls are counted but carry no tokens. One plain table set, not a chart | `public.ai_usage` (migration `20260921090000_ai_usage.sql`, service role only), `recordAiUsage` / `summarizeUsage` / `readAiUsage` in `lib/ai/ai-usage.ts` (written by `askAI`, `analyzeDashboard`, the Save probe), `AiUsageCard` on `app/(app)/settings/ai/usage/page.tsx`; Sales Activity mirrors it at Pengaturan → AI → Pemakaian |
| Integration settings: written keys, tested connections, chosen from what the endpoint reports | A connection to an outside service is one page in two cards: the connection (endpoint, key), then what it offers (models). The key field is empty on every visit and names the stored key only by its last four characters, because a secret is written and never read back (HubSpot integrations, Zapier connections, Vercel environment variables); "Test connection" makes one real request, reports the outcome as a sentence with a check or a cross, and fills the model pickers from the answer, so a person chooses among models the endpoint actually serves (Metabase database test, Grafana data sources); a picker falls back to a text field when nothing is listed, and a stored value the endpoint no longer lists stays selectable. Saving tests again so the stored status describes what was stored, and also asks each chosen model for one word, because a model can be listed and still not answer (a reasoning model given a small token budget answers with nothing); that failure belongs on the settings page with the model's stop reason in a sentence, never as a code on the dashboard. The same connection is shared with Sales Activity and shown identically there | `AiSettingsForm` in `features/settings/components/ai-settings-form.tsx` under Settings → AI, `app/actions/ai-settings-actions.ts`, `lib/ai/ai-proxy.ts`, `lib/ai/ai-settings.ts` (Vault-backed key via `fn_ai_store_key` / `fn_ai_read_key`, service role only); `lib/ai-client.ts` resolves the stored connection with the AI_* env as fallback |
| Settings index: a grouped list of destinations | Settings is a list of places to go, so it is an M3 list: subheaders name the groups (Configuration, Workspace, Adoption, Administration) with one supporting sentence each, and every page is one two-line list item at 72dp (leading icon in a 40px neutral container, headline, supporting text clamped at two lines rather than one, because a one-line clamp cut every description to a few words on a phone, trailing chevron). Grouped lists are what Android Settings, Google Account, Salesforce Setup, HubSpot, Linear and Notion use; a grid of equal cards makes the eye zig-zag and M3 keeps cards for self-contained content of mixed kinds. The chevron says the row opens a page inside the app (the ↗ arrow is only for leaving it). Icons are neutral and tint to primary on hover; no status colour decorates a row. Rows are filtered by the grant each page needs, and the inline Currency display row is drawn to the same measurements. The list stops at `max-w-4xl`, a readable line, instead of the 1200px a data table needs. The page header's subtitle collapses on scroll by animating its grid row to zero, never to a fixed height, so a subtitle is never clipped and one that wraps on a phone shows whole. Same pattern and measurements as Sales Activity's Pengaturan | `app/(app)/settings/page.tsx` (`sections`, `SettingsRow`, `SettingsAccessSkeleton`), `CurrencySettingsRow` in `features/settings/components/currency-settings-card.tsx`, `components/layout/settings-page-header.tsx` |
| Announcing a release | A release note nobody opens is not an announcement. Slack, Notion, Figma and Gmail announce a release once per person with a dialog of two or three highlights, each with a way straight to the feature, and a text button to put it off; nothing sequential, nothing on every login, never for every fix. The control is two layers, as those teams run it (highlights written with the feature, switched on from a panel without a deploy): a changelog entry may carry an `announcement` block, and Settings → Announcements lists those newest first, one row each with a Switch (48px hit area; on a phone the text wraps beside it) and an Announce again text action that asks first because it reaches every account (M3 dialog for a consequence that cannot be taken back). A release ships off unless it is everyone's business. The dialog, "What's new in LeadEngine", shows on the dashboard only, after the page settles, at most three items, so a person who went straight to a record is never interrupted; on a desk it is an M3 dialog, below `lg` (where the shell drops the sidebar) a modal bottom sheet with a scrolling body and its actions above the safe area. Later (text) and Got it (filled) both close it for good; Try it now goes to the feature; the changelog link shows only to people who may open the changelog. "Once" is once per account on every device, keyed on the release date or the last Announce again, so switching one off and on never repeats it and Announce again makes it new for everyone. `announced_at` is therefore empty until an Announce again: a switch never stamps it. One set for the whole group, not per business unit. Same rule as Sales Activity's Yang baru, without its unread dot, because LeadEngine's changelog is an admin page | `Announcement` on `ChangelogEntry` in `features/changelog/changelog-data.ts`; `announcementStates` / `pendingAnnouncements` in `lib/announcements/announcements.ts`, read by `listAnnouncements` / `pendingAnnouncementsFor` in `lib/announcements/announcement-queries.ts`; `public.release_announcements` (on/off, `announced_at`; readable by every signed-in person, written only by `setAnnouncementEnabled` / `reannounce` in `app/actions/announcement-actions.ts` with the service client after `settings.update`) and `public.user_hints` (seen marks `announce-<key>-<stamp>`, own rows only, `markHintsSeen` in `app/actions/hint-actions.ts`), migration `20260923110000_leadengine_announcements.sql`; `AnnouncementDialog` in `features/announcements/components/announcement-dialog.tsx`, rendered by `app/(app)/page.tsx`; `BottomSheet` in `components/ui/bottom-sheet.tsx`, `useCompact` in `hooks/use-compact.ts`; `AnnouncementList` on `app/(app)/settings/announcements/page.tsx` |
| Usage is one row per person per day, read by admins, never a tracker | An admin could see what changed in the CRM but not whether anyone opens it. Salesforce's Lightning Usage App, Notion's workspace analytics and Microsoft 365's active-users report answer that with the same small shape, and so does this: one row per person per WIB day (first and last seen, pages opened, the last page) and one counter per page per day. Daily rows, not an event stream: a day row answers "is this person using it", a page counter answers "which screens earn their place", and neither can be replayed into a trail of what someone did, which is the line between usage and surveillance. The browser calls the server at most once per 30 seconds and batches the pages opened in between; nothing is sent while the tab is hidden; after five minutes on one page a heartbeat marks the person as still here (last seen moves, no page is counted), but only if they tapped, typed or scrolled since the last call. Paths are normalised in the browser and again on the server (uuid, numeric and opaque segments, and the slug of a company unit or a goal, become `:id`; query string and fragment are dropped), so no record id lands in a usage table and every lead's page counts as one "Lead details"; names come from the drawer's and Settings' own words at render time, and an unknown path shows as itself. Per person across the whole workspace, not per company: sign-in has no per-unit gate, so there is no `company_id`. Reading is for admins (super_admin, admin) behind the Settings grant: row security returns rows only to `fn_user_is_admin()`, and anyone else with Settings access reads "Admins only" rather than a table of people who seem never to have come (the Settings row says "Admins only" too); a person writes only their own day row and nobody can delete one. The page reads as those products do: people active today, in 7 and in 30 days against the headcount (M365's active-users cards); "Daily active users", a bar per WIB day with every day drawn and a quiet day at 0, weekdays in the primary (the ink LeadEngine's charts give a measure; the `--chart-*` tokens here are unthemed) and weekends in the muted ink with a legend naming both, a one-line tooltip ("Tue 22 Sep · 5 people"), and the axis still drawn when nothing is recorded while "Nothing recorded yet" stays the only empty message; one line per person with Last active as a relative time and the exact WIB stamp on hover (Slack, Teams), days active in 30, pages opened in 7, and an eight-week line of days active per rolling week on a fixed 0–7 scale so rows compare; "Inactive 7 days" as a muted chip, never a status colour, and "Never" for someone never seen; everyone who can sign in is listed, seen or not, counted as Settings → Users counts them (active profiles). Then the most opened pages as a bar list with an "Other (N pages)" fold and "Show all". One period (7, 30 or 90 days, in the URL) governs the daily chart and the pages, and the same segmented control sits on each of those two cards, because the tiles and the people table count fixed windows. The page reflows to a phone: tiles in two columns, the people table as one card per person below `md`, bars never wider than their day's slot (ninety days at 360px), 44px touch targets, and no sideways page scroll, for which its root opts out of the shell's 900px content floor. Honest disclosure: the page's subtitle and the changelog say what is recorded (which page, when) and what is not (nothing typed, nothing shown on a page, no location). | A phone gets the real width only where the page is built for it | LeadEngine is desk-first: `<main>` keeps a 900px floor so the list pages, tables and the Kanban board keep their columns and scroll sideways on a phone rather than collapsing into something half-usable. | Who may see and change what | Role & Izin: modul × Lihat/Buat/Ubah/Hapus with a reach (Cakupan) under Lihat and Ubah; role chips on top, one pane, the reading guide folded. | Drawer rail vs account menu | The drawer holds the daily destinations (Dashboard, Pipeline, Companies, Contacts) and one Administration group for Settings; the profile, the changelog, the panel toggle and sign-out live behind the account menu at the foot of the drawer, on the panel's own tokens, with no focus ring left behind on close (M3 navigation drawer: 3 to 7 destinations; Slack, Notion, Linear).
